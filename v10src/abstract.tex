\begin{abstract}
This is Version~10 of the scoped O.1 cone audit and revises
XVI-O1-CONE-v9. The cone \(C_\alpha\times\RR^2\) and the
curvature-regular (CR) and Friedrichs (F) realizations are still
prescribed inputs. All v9 content is kept. The new material is
listed below with its scope (Section~\ref{sec:whatv10}). Every new
number comes from a hashed run (Appendix~\ref{app:v10artifacts}).

\emph{(1) A closed form for \(\Delta J\).} The CR \(k=-1\) mode is
derived exactly:
\(I_{\rm CR}(\mu)=\tfrac{(\mu-1)(6\mu^2+3\mu-2)}6-(\mu-1)^3(\mu+1)\psi_1(\mu-1)\),
with \(\mu=1/\alpha\). The F mode is identified by PSLQ, not derived:
\(I_{\rm F}(\mu)=\tfrac{(\mu-1)(59\mu^3-57\mu^2-62\mu+48)}{36}-(\mu-1)^3(\mu+1)\tfrac{\pi^2}6\).
The resulting \(\Delta J\) agrees with direct quadrature at nine
fresh angles to \(1.5\times10^{-52}\), and with the v9 table to
\(4.4\times10^{-27}\).

\emph{(2) The ghost normalizations are derived from tree data.}
Three tree operator ratios are each single-valued on all atoms and
momenta tested: \(\rho=-\ii\), \(\epsilon=1\) and \(\tau=-\ii/2\).
They give \(\lambda_{\rm CK}=4\rho\lambda_{\rm pred}/\epsilon=-8\)
and \(\lambda_{\rm CB}=-\tau\lambda_{\rm CK}=-4\ii\). These are the
values v9 had fitted.

\emph{(3) Chamber ranks.} The rank is constant inside every
chamber: 26 on the six \(N=34\) chambers and 29 on F above
\(\tfrac12\), so the exceptional set inside chambers is empty. F
above \(\tfrac12\) has nine cocycles at every angle. Eight are
constant vectors; the ninth turns with \(\alpha\). The argument
assumes an a priori degree bound on the row entries.

\emph{(4) The transverse-jet extension of the slice catalogue}
(new code; the loop momentum stays on the slice). At order zero it
reproduces the v8 catalogue on every key. Same-channel \(r^2\) jets
are dressed by \(\Dcal_b\). A declared Mellin--zeta scheme gives
\(\Dcal^Z_b=\Dcal_b+11\alpha/180\) exactly, so the scheme dependence
is a bulk term. The dressed values vanish at \(\alpha=1\) (F) and
satisfy CR\(=\)F at \(\alpha=\tfrac12\). The mixed \(r^2\) trace
now has a closed finite-part evaluator. The D04 jet term is
entirely mixed (pair \((1,0)\)), so its value stays \PARTIAL.

\emph{(5) Vector-key finite parts} in a declared \(k\)-\(\zeta\)
scheme. The pole residues reproduce the v9 logarithms to
\(10^{-20}\). The off-diagonal keys are zero, and the diagonal keys
are \(\pm c(\alpha)\kappa^2P_L^2\).

The 20 jet-level Ward combinations, the full 34- and 18-dimensional
projections, and the separate values of \(e_5,e_6\) remain \OPEN{}
or \PARTIAL. O.1 on the cone remains \NOTCLOSED.
\end{abstract}
