(Section~\ref{sec:whatv11}; hashes in Appendix~\ref{app:v11artifacts}):

used a Cauchy radius too large near the endpoints and for large

corrected evaluator reproduces the diagonal identity to

hypergeometric factors are incomplete Beta functions. This gives
\(\Delta J=-(\mu-1)\Gamma(\mu)^{-1}\Gamma(2-\mu)^{-1}\int_0^1(3-2x)W^2/w\).
An integration by parts and the v10 Gamma sums then give
\(I_{\rm F}\), which equals the v10 PSLQ form identically.

tracking on all 8 chambers, with 0 mismatches against the exact

In that precise scope they are
\CPIR, as the v8 four-point certificate remains.

\(\alpha\in\{\tfrac3{10},\tfrac{7071}{10000},\tfrac12,1\}\), in two

equal to scheme Z) and \(-19/180\) (\(k\)-regulator). D04 is
therefore regulator dependent and stays \PARTIAL.

Still \OPEN: the weight \(\pm1,\pm2\) and remaining mixed jet
