\emph{(1) The hidden D01 pairs are resolved, and v6 is restored.}

\(g=\Tr[e^{-(1-x)A_\mu}r^{-2}e^{-xA_\nu}]\) has a closed form: a
product of two Gauss functions, obtained from an Appell \(F_4\)

pure transverse-traceless bilinear

bilinear vanishes identically on the v6 lattice. The v6 \(hh\)
branch difference \(-\nu\kappa^2P^2f_0f_0'/(4\pi)\) is therefore
restored with a proof. It is no longer conditional.

mode sums carry \(\tfrac{\alpha}{2}(s-t)^2x(x-1)\log\Lambda\), so
absolute TT values depend on the scheme. The CR\(-\)F difference
is finite and does not depend on the regularization. We computed
